What happened?

The idea: lead-lag trading

When news moves one market, a related market can be slow to react. Buy the slow one before it catches up, and you profit when it does.

When it works

Thin markets with few traders watching can take hours to react. That's the lag you were trading.

Why it usually doesn't

  • The spread. You start behind, and the slow, thin markets have the widest spreads.
  • Already priced in. Sometimes the slow market reacts in the same hour.
  • Other news. Over 6 hours, other things move the price too.
